# Vision

## Purpose

**Praias de Portugal** helps people decide when and where to enjoy Portugal's coastline.

The application brings together environmental information, local knowledge, and beach-specific facilities into a single experience designed for beach visitors.

Rather than presenting isolated weather forecasts or lists of beaches, the application answers a simple question:

> **"Which beach should I visit today?"**

---

# Vision

Praias de Portugal aims to become the most useful source of beach information for Portugal.

The application combines:

- Weather
- Marine forecasts
- Sea temperature
- Surf conditions
- Tide predictions
- Water quality
- Beach facilities
- Accessibility
- Local services
- Maps
- Photographs
- Points of interest

The goal is to present this information in a way that is clear, fast, and immediately useful.

---

# Users

Praias de Portugal is designed for people who visit Portugal's beaches, including:

- Local residents
- Holiday visitors
- Families
- Surfers
- Walkers
- Swimmers
- Nature enthusiasts

The application should support both spontaneous beach visits and planned excursions.

---

# Principles

Praias de Portugal should be:

- Useful before being comprehensive.
- Simple without being simplistic.
- Information-rich without feeling cluttered.
- Fast to navigate.
- Easy to understand.
- Easy to extend.
- Easy to maintain.

Every feature should help users make better decisions about visiting the coast.

---

# Long-Term Goal

The application should become the natural starting point for anyone planning a visit to a Portuguese beach.

It should combine authoritative environmental information with practical local knowledge while remaining approachable, reliable, and enjoyable to use.

Over time, new information and capabilities should strengthen this purpose without changing it.
